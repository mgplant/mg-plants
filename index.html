Botanical Knowledge Base

Keep Your Plants Happy

Simple, essential guidelines to help your plants grow green and thrive in Indian weather conditions.

1. Watering Wisely

Overwatering is the #1 cause of plant death. Always test the top 1–2 inches of soil with your finger before watering. If dry, water thoroughly until it drains out from the bottom.

2. Sunlight Needs

Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect sunlight near a window. Avoid harsh afternoon Indian summer sun directly on delicate leaves to prevent scorching.

3. Well-Draining Soil

Use a light, airy soil mix containing garden soil, coco peat, and perlite or vermicompost. Ensure pot drainage holes are clear of obstructions.

4. Organic Fertilizing

Feed your plants once every 3–4 weeks during the growing season (Spring & Summer) using organic compost or liquid seaweed fertilizer.

5. Repotting Tips

Repot when roots begin growing out of bottom drainage holes, usually every 12 to 18 months. Choose a pot 1–2 inches larger than the current one.

6. Pest Protection

Wipe leaves regularly with a damp soft cloth to prevent dust buildup. Spray diluted Neem oil once a month to keep natural pests away safely.
