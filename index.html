Keep Your Plants Happy

1. Watering

Always test the top 1-2 inches of soil before watering.

2. Sunlight

Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect sunlight.

3. Soil

Use well-draining soil with coco peat and perlite.

4. Fertilizer

Feed plants every 3-4 weeks in spring/summer.

5. Repotting

Repot every 12-18 months when roots show at the bottom.

6. Pests

Wipe leaves and spray diluted Neem oil monthly.
